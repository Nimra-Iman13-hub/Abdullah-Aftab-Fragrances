Abdullah Aftab Fragrances is a premier fragrance house dedicated to the art of perfumery. We source the finest raw materials from France, the Middle East, and across the globe to create extraordinary scents that linger in memory.

Our premium perfume oils are laboratory-grade, alcohol-free, and long-lasting — designed for those who appreciate true olfactory artistry. Each formulation is a masterpiece, blending tradition with modern sophistication.

From classic Arabic accords to contemporary fine fragrances, our catalog of 800+ oils serves perfumers, retailers, and fragrance enthusiasts across Pakistan and beyond.
